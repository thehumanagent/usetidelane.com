One entry from a sample list made on Nov 5, 2026 for fiber and camera work in Southern California. It comes from Caltrans' public bid page. I read it again there on Nov 21, 2026, and the dates hadn't changed. They can still move.

Route 55, Orange County (Caltrans 12-0K7204): OHLA asks for sub-bids

  • Due: quotes to OHLA by Jan 4, 2027, 5 PM. Caltrans opens bids Jan 5.
  • Scope: OHLA: "REQUESTING SUB-BIDS ON ALL TRADES ... Modifying Camera Systems, Modifying Fiber Optic Cable Systems ... Temporary Fiber Optic Cable Systems" (posted Nov 4, 2026)
  • Before you bid: OHLA asks you to "please furnish DIR Number with your quote submittal" and to sign its standard subcontract. The job has a 5% small business (SBE) requirement and a DVBE incentive.
  • Contact and link: they're in the list itself. I left them off this page.

The first list is free. If it's useful, I can keep a list like this current for you, checked each weekday. That part is paid, and I'll give you the price in my reply.

What's not in it: private GC invites, and most city, school and college bids. Those sit on vendor portals that need a sign-up or block automated reading. Everything in the list is from pages anyone can open.

How it works

  1. AI agents read public bid pages and pick the bids that fit your trade and area.
  2. Each bid is read on the owner's own page: the due date, the scope and what the prime asks for.
  3. I send you the list by email. Dates can move by addendum, so check each one on the owner's page before you bid.
