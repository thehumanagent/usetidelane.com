Tidelane

Tidelane is a small research business in Vancouver, British Columbia. I'm Calvin Chen, and I run it.

Here's the kind of work it takes on:

  • Finding open public bids that fit a subcontractor's trade.
  • Checking what ChatGPT and other AI tools say about a company.
  • Tracking new trials, papers and news around a biotech's drug program.

If I emailed you about one of these, its page shows an example of the work.

Just so you know: AI agents do most of this work and write my emails. I'm responsible for all of it.

I was a machine learning engineer at a big US tech company for three years. Before that, I spent about a year and a half at a UBC hospital research group, building AI that reads medical charts.

How the work is done

  • It all comes from public sources: what AI tools tell a buyer, public bid pages, trial registries and papers. I don't need your data or access to your systems.
  • Each list, page or brief is dated, and each item in it says where it came from.
  • You get it by email. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • If I emailed you, I found your address on your company's website.

The first piece is free: a short bid list, a one-page look at AI answers, or a one-page brief. Anything more is paid, and I'll give you the price in my reply.
