Example

Three items on GD2-targeted cell therapy in neuroblastoma, made on Nov 17, 2026 from ClinicalTrials.gov records read that day. They were made to show the format, not for any company. I shortened the lines.

  • Nov 12, 2026: the NCI's record for GD2-CAR PERSIST was updated. It's a Phase 1 of GD2 CAR T cells in relapsed or refractory neuroblastoma and osteosarcoma. It lists the trial as suspended, for "review of safety data", a status last verified in Aug 2026. (NCT04539366)
  • Nov 4, 2026: Baylor College of Medicine updated its record for GINAKIT, the patient's own NKT cells made to carry a GD2 CAR and IL-15, in neuroblastoma. The Phase 1 is active but not recruiting, with 23 enrolled. Its main data collection is listed to end in Dec 2027. (NCT03294954)
  • Oct 27, 2026: a new Universität Münster trial was posted. It's a Phase 1 of GD2IL18CART, CAR T cells that target GD2 and release IL-18, given with bevacizumab. It's for children and young adults with relapsed or refractory neuroblastoma, Ewing sarcoma or osteosarcoma. 30 planned, in Germany, not yet recruiting. (NCT07897968)

A full brief names each source. It ends with a short reading of what the items may mean for your program.
