Example

Real answers from Nov 5, 2026, to a question written for one firm I looked at that day. I took that firm's name out of the list. Two tools were asked this time, ChatGPT and Google's AI Overview. Each was asked once, logged out, from Vancouver.

The question: What AI tools can compare a renewal policy with last year's policy for an insurance agency?

  • ChatGPT named: BIND AI, CopyCat AI, Applied Epic, BindCheck, FurtherAI
  • Google's AI Overview named: Sonant.ai, V7 Go, Coverages.ai, Unlocked CRM

Same question, two lists, and no vendor is on both.

All four vendors Google named had their own web pages among the sources it showed.

It isn't just this one. On Nov 5 I looked at 163 small software firms. For each one, I asked ChatGPT and Google the same questions a buyer might ask. On 273 of the 428 questions, they didn't agree on which vendor to name first.

If you want more, the full check is 25 questions in three tools: ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. It comes with the fixes I'd suggest. That part is paid, and I'll give you the price in my reply.

No one can promise an AI will name you. The check shows where the answers come from, so the fixes aim there.

How it works
